A kitchen with a story

Small oven.
Big-hearted bakes.

Sweet Crumbs began with one tiny oven, a well-loved recipe book, and a slightly unreasonable number of vanilla beans. Today, we're still a small neighborhood bakery—and still believe the best things are made slowly, by hand, and shared.

We bake with seasonal fruit, good butter, and ingredients we can pronounce. No shortcuts, just the sort of treats that make an ordinary Tuesday feel a little more special.
